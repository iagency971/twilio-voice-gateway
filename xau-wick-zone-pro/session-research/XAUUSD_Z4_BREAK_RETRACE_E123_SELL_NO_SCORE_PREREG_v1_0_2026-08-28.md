## Original-price-space SELL mechanics

The transformed BUY engine is equivalent to the following SELL rules in original price space:

1. **Main Z4 bearish break**
   - the causal lower Z4 is known from the state preceding the M1 close;
   - a breakout occurs when the previous M1 close is at/above `main_zlo` and the current confirmed M1 close is strictly below `main_zlo`;
   - the breakout candle itself cannot count as the later retracement candle.

2. **Frozen target**
   - at breakout, freeze the next lower causal Z4;
   - TP is the first touch of that lower target Z4 at its **upper boundary `target_zhi`**;
   - if no lower Z4 exists at breakout, the episode has no target and is not executable.

3. **Mandatory retracement**
   - after breakout, an M1 range must intersect the broken main Z4 at least by wick before an E trigger can fire.

4. **Structural invalidation**
   - wick above `main_zhi` is allowed;
   - only a confirmed M1 close strictly above frozen `main_zhi` invalidates the setup before or after entry.

5. **E1 / E2 / E3**
   - use the current causal sticky E-SELL zones generated by the mirrored frozen E architecture;
   - E may be entirely inside the main Z4, overlap it, lie fully below it, or lie fully above it;
   - touching E is by M1 range intersection;
   - E rank is unchanged under reflection: E1 is the first/nearest frozen directional entry slot, then E2, then E3.

6. **Bearish rejection trigger**
   - exact mirror of legacy BR70;
   - `close < open`;
   - `(high - close) / (high - low) >= 0.70` for non-zero range;
   - equivalently, the close is in the lower 30% of the M1 range.

7. **Execution**
   - SELL at next M1 open, still inside the selected session;
   - original entry must remain structurally between target and invalidation (`target_zhi < entry < main_zhi`).

8. **Outcome ordering**
   - TP if M1 low first touches/crosses `target_zhi`;
   - invalidation if confirmed M1 close first rises strictly above `main_zhi`;
   - same-M1 TP + invalidation is `AMBIGUOUS`;
   - if neither occurs before session end, `NEITHER`.
