# XAUUSD Reaction Zone Research — Phase C v2 Decision Gate

Frozen before reading the completed 2011–2025 Phase-C v2 aggregate.

R2 `RECLAIM_PULLBACK` is an extension of the preregistered entry family and is evaluated under exactly the same survival conditions as the Phase-C v1 cells. It cannot loosen or retroactively alter the v1 benchmark.

A `sample × entry_model × target_R` cell survives only if all are true:

1. total trades >= 300;
2. weighted average net R after the historical $22 RT/100oz cost sensitivity > 0;
3. annual median net R after $22 > 0;
4. at least 10 of 15 years have positive net R after $22;
5. weighted average net R under 1.5x costs ($33 RT/100oz) > 0;
6. at least 8 of 15 years remain positive under 1.5x costs;
7. median annual PF net after $22 > 1.0.

No isolated RR bin is selected merely because it maximizes the surface. Neighboring-R stability is inspected. Passing this gate nominates a candidate for stricter chronology / broker / prospective validation; it is not a live-profitability validation.
