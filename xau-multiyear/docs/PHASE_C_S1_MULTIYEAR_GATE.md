## Promoted cells from the 2025 screen

- sample: `DISPLACEMENT_ORIGIN_ONLY`
- entry model: `TOUCH_NEXT_OPEN`

The 2025 screen that nominated these cells was deliberately permissive: positive net expectancy after the historical $22 RT/100oz cost sensitivity, no worse than -0.02R under 1.5x costs, and neighboring-R support. Promotion is not validation.

## Multiyear survival gate

Each promoted target-R cell survives only if all are true across 2011–2025:

1. total trades >= 300;
2. weighted average net R after $22 RT/100oz > 0;
3. annual median net R after $22 > 0;
4. at least 10 of 15 years have positive net R after $22;
5. weighted average net R under 1.5x costs ($33 RT/100oz) > 0;
6. at least 8 of 15 years remain positive under 1.5x costs;
7. median annual PF net after $22 > 1.0.

Additional interpretation rule: neighboring-R stability is required qualitatively. A single isolated passing RR without support from either adjacent promoted RR is not accepted as a robust candidate.

## Scientific status

This is still exploratory historical research. Passing this gate only nominates a candidate for stricter chronology, broker/feed replication, and prospective validation. It is not evidence of live profitability.
